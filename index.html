FAQ

Good questions

How early should I book bridal makeup?

Two to four weeks ahead is best, and earlier for peak wedding season. A trial look can be booked before the date.

Is the parlour really ladies-only?

Yes. Only women staff and women clients, so you can relax fully.

How do you keep things hygienic?

Tools are sanitised after every client, and we use fresh disposables for waxing and facials.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free. Booking ahead guarantees your slot, especially on weekends.

How can I pay?

Cash and UPI. Ask us about other options when you book.

Do you offer home service?

Ask us on call about bridal home visits. Availability depends on the date and location.
