FAQ

Good to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, message us on WhatsApp or call. We confirm your slot by message. Walk-ins are welcome when a stylist is free.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please tell us at least 24 hours ahead so we can offer your slot to someone else. You can reschedule free of charge.

Do you offer a bridal trial?

Yes. Gold and Platinum include a trial. You can add one to Silver for a small fee. Book your trial three to four weeks before the wedding.

Which products do you use?

Professional-grade products, and we tell you what goes on your skin and hair. Mention any allergies when you book and we will patch-test first.

How do you keep things hygienic?

Tools are cleaned and sterilised between clients. Towels are freshly laundered, and wax strips, files and buffers are single-use.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash and all major debit and credit cards. Package bookings need a deposit to hold your date.

Can I walk in without a booking?

Yes, subject to availability. Booking ahead guarantees your preferred stylist and time, especially on weekends and in wedding season.
